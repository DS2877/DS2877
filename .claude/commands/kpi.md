⚠️ Read discovery numbers correctly: Roblox's ranking signals count **only** users who joined organically from Recommended for You. During a recruitment push the Home Recommendations dashboard will look flat while Audience Reach climbs — that is expected. Judge eligibility by Audience Reach and ranking by Home Recommendations, never the other way round.
