# STORE-PAGE.md

Phase 1.1. The game page is where play-through rate is won or lost — and play-through rate is the **most heavily weighted** discovery signal Roblox publishes.

---

## 1. Title

**`Fuse a Nomling`** — reasoning and the 5-title shortlist in `docs/TITLES.md`.

**With an update tag** during events, prefixed so it reads first on a phone:

```
[❄️ FROSTY] Fuse a Nomling
[🌪️ NOM STORM] Fuse a Nomling
[🆕 4 NEW] Fuse a Nomling
```

**Rules:** one tag, at the front, max ~10 characters inside the brackets, always naming *content*. Never a monetary tag — "FREE COINS", "REWARDS", "CODES" all trip the giveaway penalty (`docs/MARKETING.md` §6).

---

## 2. Icon

One character, one emotion, readable at 60 px.

**Concept A — recommended.** A single absurd fused Nomling, front-on, mouth open in delighted surprise, on a flat saturated background. Not a base Nomling — a *fusion*, so the icon itself is the pitch: "what is that thing?"

**Concept B.** Two small Nomlings at the edges with a bright burst between them and the silhouette of something new emerging. Tells the mechanic, but silhouettes read poorly at 60 px.

**Concept C.** A big-eyed Taco Tiger, cute and clean. Safest, most generic, least differentiating — it would look like fifty other games' icons, which is precisely the non-unique-metadata risk.

**Test every candidate at 60 px on a phone before choosing.** If it is unreadable small, it does not matter how good it looks large.

## 3. Thumbnails

Up to 10; the first three carry almost all the weight. Thumbnail personalization is available for the home page — every variant must still show the real game.

| # | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A fusion reveal at its peak — new creature centre, name banner readable, confetti | The hook, in one image |
| 2 | The **World First** global banner with a creature nobody has seen | The thing no competitor has |
| 3 | A plot full of varied Nomlings on pedestals, coins popping | "This is what you build" |
| 4 | A snatch chase mid-bubble | Friendly chaos, visibly non-violent |
| 5 | A Nomzilla towering over a plot | Spectacle |
| 6 | The Fusion Book, part-filled | Collection depth |

**Hard rules:** no text promising rewards; no UI mockups that don't exist; no rendered art that isn't in-game. Roblox deprioritizes mismatched metadata and the Creator Dashboard flags it.

## 4. Description

Short, honest, front-loaded — most players read only the first line.

```
Hatch silly snack-animals. Fuse two together and get a creature that has
never existed — with a name nobody has heard.

🥚 Hatch Nomlings: taco tigers, sushi pups, waffle walruses
🧪 Fuse any two and see what comes out. 876 creatures to discover.
🏆 World First: be the first player anywhere to make one — your name stays
   on it forever
🌈 Weather mutations, giant Nomzillas, and Golden Eggs
😈 Sneak into a friend's base and grab a Nomling. Bubble them to get it back.
🔁 Re-Nom to grow faster, forever

Plays great on phone. No chat needed.
```

**Why "876 creatures" rather than "thousands":** it is true, it is specific, and specificity is more persuasive than hype. The inflated number would also be mismatched metadata.

**"No chat needed"** is aimed at parents as much as players, and it is a genuine differentiator for a Kids/Select-targeted game.

## 5. Trailer — 30 seconds or less

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Cold open, no logo: two Nomlings drop into the Fusion Lab. **Hook before branding.** |
| 3–6 s | The machine shakes. A beat of anticipation. |
| 6–10 s | **The reveal.** New creature, name banner, confetti, TTS says the name. |
| 10–13 s | The World First banner sweeps across. |
| 13–18 s | Fast cuts: three more fusions, three more absurd creatures. |
| 18–22 s | A snatch chase ending in a bubble save — one laugh. |
| 22–26 s | A plot full of Nomlings, coins everywhere, a Nomzilla stomps in. |
| 26–30 s | Title card: **Fuse a Nomling**. |

**Shot for vertical first** — TikTok/Shorts is where it will mostly be seen; the horizontal cut is a crop of the same material. Must work with the sound off: every beat carries a readable caption.

## 6. Genre and metadata

- **Genre:** Simulator (secondary: Adventure). Genre is separate from the maturity questionnaire — answer the questionnaire honestly regardless of genre (`docs/COMPLIANCE.md`).
- **Social links:** the Community group, TikTok and YouTube, via the game page's approved link fields. **Never a link inside the game.**
- **Experience Events:** set up for every Nom Storm and seasonal event — up to 5 thumbnails and opt-in notifications, which is free reach.
