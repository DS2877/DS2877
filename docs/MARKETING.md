# MARKETING.md

Phase 1.1. How the game gets found, and the one structural fact that changes the whole plan.

---

## 1. Two funnels, not one

⚠️ **The most important marketing fact from Phase 0.** Roblox's own discovery documentation states that in the *ranking* stage of Recommended for You, it **"doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads, curation, friends, search, social media, or any other source."** Every ranking signal is computed on users who joined **organically from Recommended for You**.

Those other sources still matter — they "accelerate your consideration" in the *retrieval* stage. So recruitment gets us looked at; only organic cohorts decide how far we go.

**This splits the work in two:**

| | Funnel A — Eligibility | Funnel B — Ranking |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | 250 highly engaged plays in 60 days, to unlock Kids/Select | Climb Home Recommendations |
| **Tools** | invited friends, Discord/Reddit, sponsored ads, share links | retention, session depth, co-play, spend |
| **Scoreboard** | **Audience Reach** dashboard | **Home Recommendations** signals |
| **Timeline** | M5, a sprint | continuous, forever |

**Judging one by the other's scoreboard produces exactly the wrong conclusion.** During the M5 push, Home Recommendations will look flat while Audience Reach climbs. That is success, not failure.

⚠️ And a "highly engaged player" for Funnel A must meet bars on account tenure, playtime **and Roblox-wide spend** ($9.99+ in 60 days for the spend route). Recruiting fresh alt accounts cannot work — it is designed not to.

## 2. What we actually sell

Not "fuse your pets". The market leader does that at 1.7M concurrent players (`docs/TITLES.md`).

> **"The creatures are generated, not listed."**
> Every other pet game has an index you can read. Ours has creatures nobody has ever seen — including us.

Three lines that work, in priority order:
1. **"Nobody has ever made this one before."** — World First, the strongest thing we have.
2. **"What did I just make?!"** — the reveal moment.
3. **"876 creatures. You haven't seen most of them."** — honest, specific, and specificity beats hype.

**Never claim "infinite" or "millions of creatures".** The real numbers are 876 species and 6,132 book entries. Roblox penalizes mismatched metadata, kids notice exaggeration instantly, and the true number is impressive enough.

## 3. Pre-launch (from M1)

- **Reserve the name now** on TikTok and YouTube. Free, and it is lost once someone else takes it.
- **Create the Roblox Community (group)** — it owns the game and is the best channel to the eventual player base (Philip task 3).
- **Devlog clips from the phone.** Short, vertical, one idea each: a creature that came out ridiculous, a name the generator produced, a fusion that failed. Devlog audiences are small but they are the first 250 plays.
- **Creator Mode is a growth feature, not a cosmetic.** It is in M4 for this reason (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## 4. The M5 push — 250 highly engaged plays

Target: clear the bar inside 60 days of the Dec 8–12 launch. The holiday break sits inside that window, which is the single biggest advantage of the later date.

| Channel | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | Invite age-checked 16+ friends and colleagues | They must be age-checked. Self-declared 16 counts as Select, not Roblox. |
| **Reddit** | r/roblox, r/robloxgamedev and similar | Follow each subreddit's self-promotion rules exactly. One bad post costs the channel. |
| **Discord** | Roblox dev and player communities | Same. |
| **Sponsored ads** | Small campaign via Ads Manager | Cheapest way to get in front of age-checked 16+. Budget is Philip's call — brief §11.3 means Claude never spends money. |
| **Share links** | Always share the game's share link | The only attribution path for Audience Expansion later. |
| **Daily** | Check Audience Reach | Stalling 3+ days means the funnel is broken, not slow. |

**Watch bounce rate obsessively during this window.** If `<60 s` bounce is high, stop recruiting and fix the FTUE — sending more people into a leaky first minute burns the channel and teaches the algorithm the wrong thing.

## 5. After eligibility (M6 onward)

- **Content: 1–2 short videos a day.** Fusion reveals, World Firsts, snatch saves, Nomzilla. Each must read in a vertical crop, run under 10 seconds, and make sense with the sound off (`docs/GDD.md` §8).
- **Events:** weekend Nom Storms at fixed times (`docs/LIVEOPS.md`).
- **Community:** codes on socials at content moments; outreach to Video Stars creators once there is something worth filming.
- **Discovery surfaces:** game-page videos for the Moments feed; Experience Events with up to 5 thumbnails and opt-in notifications; browser-play links when they ship.

## 6. Things that actively reduce our reach

Verified in Roblox's discovery docs. Each is a trap that looks like marketing:

| Trap | Why it hurts |
|---|---|
| **Leading with giveaways** | Metadata implying monetary reward is deprioritized. A "FREE COINS!!" title tag costs more than it earns. |
| **Mismatched metadata** | Thumbnails must show real gameplay. Also just dishonest. |
| **Non-unique metadata** | Titles and visuals resembling existing games rank lower. ⚠️ **Our biggest exposure** — we are entering a crowded, heavily-cloned genre. Original art direction and the unique noun "Nomling" are discovery requirements, not taste. |
| **Rewarding likes / favourites / group joins** | Against Community Standards; check and log before any such reward. |

Content quality is **reclassified with every update**, and affected games show a daily-updating banner on the Creator Dashboard — so a mistake here is recoverable, but only if we notice it.
